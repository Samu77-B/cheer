What we do

Programmes in rooms, halls and care settings.

CHEER and its community arts project, My Band Sabula, will deliver a range of community-based programmes. The photographs and film on this page are temporary stand-ins until CHEER’s own media is ready.

Arts, music and cultural workshops

Performances and skills-based sessions that inform, inspire and engage people of all ages.

Youth development and mentoring

Creative skills programmes that build confidence, employability and resilience.

Community concerts and outreach

Visits planned for schools, care homes and community centres. Placeholder film.

Wellbeing for adults and older people

Social and creative activities that support mental, emotional and social health.

Intergenerational projects

Shared learning that builds connection between younger and older neighbours.

Training and volunteering

Mentoring and volunteering routes for community members who want to give time.

CHEER Organisation Ltd

My Band Sabula operates under CHEER as a community arts initiative supporting outreach, education, mentoring, and engagement for public benefit.
