What we do

Programmes

CHEER and its community arts project, My Band Sabula, will deliver a range of community-based programmes.

Arts, music, and culture

Arts, music, and cultural workshops and performances.

Youth development

Youth development, mentoring, and creative skills programmes.

Community concerts and outreach

Community concerts and outreach activities in schools, care homes, and community centres.

Wellbeing for adults and older people

Wellbeing, social, and creative activities for adults and older people.

Intergenerational projects

Intergenerational projects that build connection and shared learning.

Training and volunteering

Training, mentoring, and volunteering opportunities for community members.

My Band Sabula supports outreach, education, mentoring, and engagement as part of this work.

Concerts and other ticketed events are sold through UrNextEvent. Many community activities are free to attend and will not appear there.
