CHEER Organisation Ltd

Community, Health, Educational entertainment & Empowerment for Resilience

CHEER and its community arts project, My Band Sabula, offer inclusive and accessible activities that improve education, wellbeing, and social connection.

See our activities Get involved

What we do

We deliver community-based programmes through creative, musical, cultural, and skills-based activities for people of all ages.

Arts and culture

Workshops, performances, and cultural activities that advance the arts as a means of personal development, social inclusion, and community cohesion.

Health and wellbeing

Inclusive arts, music, and community programmes that promote mental, emotional, and social wellbeing.

Connection

Opportunities for social interaction and community participation, including work that relieves social isolation and loneliness.

My Band Sabula

My Band Sabula operates under CHEER as a community arts initiative supporting outreach, education, mentoring, and engagement activities for public benefit.
