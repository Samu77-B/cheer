Building resilience through connection

CHEER and My Band Sabula offer inclusive, accessible activities that improve education, wellbeing, and social connection — through arts, music, and community programmes for people of all ages.

Our impact

The charity addresses isolation, limited opportunities for young people, poor mental and emotional wellbeing, and a lack of accessible creative engagement.

See our purposes

Education

Educational entertainment that informs, inspires, and engages through creative, musical, cultural, and skills-based activities.

Health & wellbeing

Inclusive arts, music, and community programmes that promote mental, emotional, and social wellbeing.

Social connection

Opportunities for social interaction and community participation, including work that relieves loneliness.

Youth support

Positive activities that help young people build confidence, life skills, employability, and resilience.
