Community arts

My Band Sabula

My Band Sabula operates under CHEER as a community arts initiative supporting outreach, education, mentoring, and engagement activities for public benefit.

Programmes sit within CHEER’s wider work: concerts and outreach, educational entertainment, and mentoring that help people take part and stay connected.

See programmes

Music is how we express ourselves, connect with others and imagine a better tomorrow.

Outreach

Community concerts and outreach activities in schools, care homes, and community centres.

Education

Educational entertainment through creative, musical, cultural, and skills-based activities for people of all ages.

Mentoring and engagement

Mentoring, creative skills, and engagement that help people take part, build confidence, and stay connected.
