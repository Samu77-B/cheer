About us

A charity grounded in people, culture and care.

Community, Health, Educational entertainment & Empowerment for Resilience. We bring people together through creative, musical and skills-based programmes for every age.

Our work is for public benefit: relieving isolation, opening doors for young people, and making arts and culture accessible in schools, care homes and community centres.
